Rules are very simple

Rock Paper Scissors is a simple hand game usually played between two people.

  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • If both players choose the same option, it’s a tie!

Click on Rock, Paper, or Scissors to make your choice. The computer will randomly choose its option, and the winner will be decided based on the rules above. Good luck!
